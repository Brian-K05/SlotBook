# SlotBook — visual contract

## Architecture (must)

No app sidebar. Full-bleed month/week calendar is the home. Booking happens on the calendar, not in a nested form page first.

## Type

Fraunces or Newsreader (serif headlines) + Source Sans. Warm, editorial.

## Color

Cream #f3efe7, ink #1c1917, terracotta #b45309. No zinc-amber DeskFlow look.

## Inspired by (do not copy)

Cal.com’s calm booking focus — not their logo, component kit, or marketing site clone.

## Anti-patterns

Left dark sidebar dashboards, window-chrome cards like the portfolio Work section.

## Also forbidden

- Portfolio ink theme (Syne + #5b8cff + window chrome cards)
- Repeating another sprint project’s grid or chrome
- Generic “Bootstrap dashboard” kits as the whole UI
